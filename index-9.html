About ITIAM

Identity is at the heart of modern IT.

Specialized Identity & Access Management consulting from architecture through implementation.

ITIAM provides specialized Identity & Access Management consulting focused on building secure, scalable and maintainable identity environments.

Founded by Philip Benjaminsson, ITIAM combines hands-on enterprise IAM experience with a strong specialization in One Identity Manager.

What we do

IAM expertise from architecture to implementation

We work with Identity & Access Management solutions across the full journey from requirements and architecture to technical implementation, integration and ongoing development.

Our strongest area of specialization is One Identity Manager, with expertise spanning architecture, configuration, customization, workflows, development, integrations and identity lifecycle management.

This includes designing solutions that connect people, systems, applications and organizational structures in a secure and manageable way.

We also work with joiner, mover and leaver processes, access management, automated provisioning and the integration of IAM with surrounding enterprise systems.
